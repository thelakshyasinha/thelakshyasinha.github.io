Pulse

Deloitte India · Strategy, Risk & Transactions Jun – Aug 2026 Built solo

Compliance teams need to know what changed at every regulator they answer to, every single day. Doing that by hand means nine websites, a pile of PDFs in different formats, and an analyst's morning gone before the real work starts.

Pulse collects the previous day's public updates from SEBI, MAS, SFC, HKMA, ASIC, FCA, FINRA, SEC and CFTC — typically 50 or more on a working day. It parses the documents behind each listing, pulls out entities and amounts, converts local currency to USD, runs its own quality checks, and writes an Excel pack with a dashboard, the detailed rows, and a source link on every row so nothing has to be taken on trust. Work that used to take a full morning by hand now runs before anyone sits down.

A quiet day is a real answer. Zero rows means nothing happened — not that the run failed.

The hardest part wasn't the scraping. It was pinning down the logic: deciding what counts as an update worth reporting when nine regulators publish in nine different shapes, and making that judgement hold consistently enough to trust unattended. Two months, start to finish.
